Is Your Boiler Letting You Down?

There is nothing worse than stepping into a cold shower on a chilly Nairobi morning.

🌡️

Inconsistent Temperature

Water runs hot then cold. Pressure fluctuates. This often indicates sediment buildup or a failing heating element.

💸

Rising Energy Bills

An inefficient boiler works harder to produce the same heat. If your bills are climbing, your boiler may be the culprit.

🔊

Strange Noises or Leaks

Banging, whistling, or water pooling around the base are serious warning signs. Ignoring them can lead to complete failure.
