Privacy Policy.

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains how Aqua Plumbing Solutions collects, uses, and protects your personal information.

Last Updated: January 2024

1. Who We Are

Aqua Plumbing Solutions ("we," "us," or "our") is a professional plumbing and maintenance company serving Nairobi, Machakos, and Kajiado counties in Kenya. We are committed to protecting the privacy and security of our clients, website visitors, and business partners.

This Privacy Policy applies to information we collect through:

  • Our website
  • WhatsApp communications (0702 555 093)
  • Email correspondence
  • Phone calls
  • In-person service visits
  • Any other interaction with our brand

By using our services or interacting with our platforms, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide Directly

  • Name: To address you properly and confirm your identity.
  • Phone Number (WhatsApp): To communicate with you about bookings, quotes, and service updates.
  • Location / Neighborhood: To dispatch the nearest technician and calculate response times.
  • Property Address: To arrive at your location for service delivery.
  • Email Address: For corporate clients, invoicing, and formal documentation.
  • Service Details: Descriptions of plumbing issues, photos you share, and service history.
  • Payment Information: We do not store your M-Pesa or bank details. Payments are processed through official channels.

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Website Usage Data: Pages visited, time spent, and general browsing behavior (via cookies and analytics tools).
  • Device Information: Browser type, operating system, and approximate location (city-level only).
  • WhatsApp Metadata: Delivery status, read receipts, and timestamps.

2.3 Information We Do NOT Collect

  • National ID numbers or passport details (unless required for corporate contracts).
  • Bank account numbers or credit card details.
  • Biometric data.
  • Health information.
  • Data from children under 18 years old.

3. How We Use Your Information

3.1 Service Delivery

  • To respond to your enquiries and bookings.
  • To dispatch technicians to your location.
  • To prepare quotes and invoices.
  • To maintain service records and warranty documentation.

3.2 Communication

  • To send booking confirmations via WhatsApp.
  • To provide technician name, photo, and ETA updates.
  • To follow up after service completion to ensure satisfaction.
  • To respond to questions, complaints, or feedback.

3.3 Business Operations

  • To process payments and issue receipts.
  • To maintain accurate financial records (as required by Kenyan law).
  • To analyze service demand and improve our coverage areas.
  • To train our staff on customer service standards.

3.4 Legal Compliance

  • To comply with tax regulations and business registration requirements.
  • To respond to lawful requests from government authorities.
  • To protect our legal rights in the event of disputes.

3.5 Marketing (With Your Consent)

  • To send occasional service reminders or maintenance tips via WhatsApp.
  • To inform you of special offers or new services.
  • You can opt out of marketing messages at any time by replying "STOP" on WhatsApp.

4. How We Protect Your Information

4.1 Technical Safeguards

  • WhatsApp communications are protected by end-to-end encryption.
  • Our website uses HTTPS encryption (SSL certificate).
  • We limit access to client data to only those employees who need it.

4.2 Operational Safeguards

  • Our technicians are trained on client confidentiality.
  • Physical documents (if any) are stored in locked cabinets.
  • Digital records are password-protected and accessible only to authorized personnel.

4.3 Data Retention

  • Service records and invoices: Retained for 7 years (as required by Kenyan tax law).
  • WhatsApp conversations: Retained for as long as necessary to provide service and maintain warranties.
  • Website analytics data: Retained for 26 months.

4.4 Data Breach Response

In the unlikely event of a data breach, we will notify affected clients within 72 hours of discovery, as required by Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019.

5. Sharing Your Information

5.1 We May Share Information With

  • Our Technicians: Only the name, address, and service details needed to complete the job.
  • Payment Processors: M-Pesa and banking partners process payments directly.
  • Legal Authorities: Only when required by law, court order, or to protect our legal rights.

5.2 We Do NOT Share Information With

  • Third-party marketing companies.
  • Data brokers.
  • Other clients.
  • Social media platforms (unless you interact with us publicly).

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Ever.

6. Your Rights (Under Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019)

As a Kenyan citizen or resident, you have specific rights regarding your personal information:

  • Right to Access: You can request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification: You can ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Right to Erasure: You can request that we delete your personal information, subject to legal retention requirements.
  • Right to Object: You can object to certain uses of your data, such as marketing.
  • Right to Data Portability: You can request your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Where processing is based on consent, you can withdraw it at any time.

To Exercise Your Rights: Contact us via WhatsApp at 0702 555 093 or email info@aquaplumbing.co.ke. We will respond within 30 days as required by law.

7. Cookies & Website Tracking

Our website uses cookies to improve your browsing experience and understand how visitors interact with our content.

Types of Cookies We Use

  • Essential Cookies: Required for the website to function properly.
  • Analytics Cookies: Help us understand which pages are most useful (via Google Analytics).
  • Preference Cookies: Remember your language or location preferences.

Managing Cookies: You can disable cookies in your browser settings. However, some website features may not function properly without them.

8. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites (such as Google Maps or social media platforms). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these external sites. We encourage you to review their privacy policies before providing any personal information.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. When we make significant changes, we will post a notice on our website and update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page.
