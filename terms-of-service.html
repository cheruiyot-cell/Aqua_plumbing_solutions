2. Our Services

Aqua Plumbing Solutions provides professional plumbing and maintenance services including, but not limited to:

  • Bathroom remodeling
  • Boiler installation and repair
  • Water treatment and filtration
  • Leak detection and repair
  • Commercial facilities maintenance

All services are performed by certified, insured technicians using professional-grade materials and equipment.

3. Bookings & Scheduling

3.1 Booking Confirmation

  • Bookings are confirmed via WhatsApp or phone.
  • We provide an estimated arrival window at the time of booking.
  • For emergencies within Nairobi, we guarantee arrival within 90 minutes.

3.2 Technician Identity

  • We provide the name and photo of your assigned technician before arrival.
  • Our technicians arrive in branded uniforms and carry identification.

3.3 Access Requirements

  • You (or an authorized representative) must be present at the property during service delivery.
  • If access is denied upon arrival, a call-out fee may apply (see Section 6).

3.4 Cancellations & Rescheduling

  • You may cancel or reschedule at no cost with at least 2 hours' notice.
  • For scheduled maintenance or larger projects, 24 hours' notice is appreciated.
  • Repeated no-shows or last-minute cancellations may require a deposit for future bookings.

4. Quotes & Pricing

4.1 Digital Quotes

  • We provide itemized quotes via WhatsApp before work begins.
  • Quotes include labor, materials, and any applicable call-out or travel fees.
  • You must approve the quote before work commences.

4.2 Pricing Accuracy

4.3 Payment Terms

  • Residential clients: Payment is due upon completion of work.
  • Corporate clients: 30-day invoicing available with approved credit.
  • Larger projects: May require a deposit (typically 30-50%) before commencement.

4.4 Accepted Payment Methods

  • M-Pesa (Paybill or Till)
  • Bank transfer
  • Cash (for smaller jobs)

  • All labor performed by Aqua Plumbing Solutions technicians.

  • Repairs, installations, and replacements.

5.2 Warranty Period

12 months from the date of service completion.

5.3 What Is NOT Covered

  • Damage caused by misuse, neglect, or third-party interference.
  • Issues caused by pre-existing plumbing problems unrelated to our work.
  • Normal wear and tear.
  • Fixtures or materials supplied by the client.
  • Work performed by other plumbers after our service.

5.4 How to Claim

  • Contact us via WhatsApp at 0702 555 093 with a description of the issue.
  • We will assess the situation and, if covered, dispatch a technician to resolve it free of charge.

6. Call-Out Fees & Travel Charges

6.1 Call-Out Fee

  • A standard dispatch fee applies to emergency call-outs.
  • This fee covers technician travel and initial assessment.
  • The fee is communicated clearly before you confirm the booking.
  • The fee is waived if you proceed with the repair.

6.2 Travel Charges

  • Within Nairobi: No additional travel fee.
  • Machakos and Kajiado counties: A modest travel fee may apply depending on distance. This is included in your quote.

6.3 No-Access Fee
