Pricing & Payment

We don't believe in vague pricing. Every job is different, so we provide a transparent, itemized quote via WhatsApp before any work begins. You'll see exactly what you're paying for—labor, materials, and any applicable call-out fees. You approve the quote before we touch a single tool. No surprises. No hidden costs.

For emergency call-outs within Nairobi, a standard dispatch fee applies (KSh 1,500 – 2,500). This covers the technician's travel and initial assessment. The fee is waived if you proceed with the repair. For scheduled maintenance and larger projects, quotes are free.

Within Nairobi County, there is never a travel fee. For Machakos and Kajiado counties, a modest travel charge may apply depending on your specific location. This is always included in your digital quote upfront.

We accept M-Pesa (Paybill or Till), bank transfers, and cash. For corporate clients, we offer 30-day invoicing with proper documentation. You'll receive a digital receipt for every payment.
