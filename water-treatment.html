What's Really in Your Water?

You turn on the tap and water comes out. It looks clear. But is it clean? Is it soft? Is it safe?

🪨

Hard Water

High mineral content causes limescale buildup in pipes, reduces appliance lifespan, leaves spots on dishes, and dries out your skin and hair.

🦠

Contaminants & Sediment

Municipal water can pick up rust, sediment, and biological contaminants. Borehole water often has high iron, fluoride, or bacterial content.

📊

Inconsistent Quality

Water quality can vary by season, location, and source. What was safe last month may not be safe today. Regular testing is essential.

Reverse Osmosis (RO) Systems

The gold standard for drinking water. RO removes up to 99% of contaminants including heavy metals, fluoride, bacteria, and dissolved solids.

Best For: Drinking water, cooking, baby formula.

Water Softening Systems

Protect your appliances, fixtures, and skin. Softeners remove calcium and magnesium—the minerals responsible for limescale and hard water damage.

Best For: Borehole water, high-rise apartments, hotels.
